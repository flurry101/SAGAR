# Marine Glossary

## Sea Surface Temperature (SST)
The temperature of the ocean surface, typically measured in degrees Celsius. SST is important for fishing because many fish species prefer specific temperature ranges. SST fronts (boundaries between warm and cold water) are often rich in fish.

## Chlorophyll-a Concentration
A measure of phytoplankton density in the ocean, expressed in mg/m³. High chlorophyll indicates productive waters where the food chain supports fish populations. Satellite sensors detect chlorophyll through ocean color analysis.

## Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ)
An area identified by satellite remote sensing as having favorable conditions for fish aggregation, based on SST patterns and chlorophyll concentration. Issued by INCOIS for Indian waters.

## Harmful Algal Bloom (HAB)
An overgrowth of algae in water that can produce toxins harmful to fish, shellfish, and humans. HABs can cause fish kills and make seafood unsafe for consumption. ORCA monitors HAB risk using satellite data.

## Wave Height (Significant)
The average height of the highest one-third of waves, measured in meters. This is the standard measure used in marine forecasts and is the primary safety metric in SVAS calculations.

## Swell
Long-period waves generated by distant storms. Swell can travel thousands of kilometers and arrive at a coast even when local weather is calm. Swell height and direction affect vessel stability.

## Beaufort Scale
A scale from 0-12 that classifies wind speed and sea state:
- 0: Calm (< 1 km/h)
- 3: Gentle breeze (12-19 km/h, small waves)
- 6: Strong breeze (39-49 km/h, large waves)
- 8: Gale (62-74 km/h, very high waves)
- 12: Hurricane (> 118 km/h)

## Bathymetry
The measurement of water depth in oceans. Important for fishing (some species prefer specific depths) and vessel safety (shallow water can cause grounding).

## EEZ (Exclusive Economic Zone)
A maritime zone extending 200 nautical miles from a country's coastline within which the country has exclusive rights to marine resources. Indian fishers must stay within India's EEZ.

## MPA (Marine Protected Area)
A designated area of ocean where human activity is restricted to protect marine ecosystems. Fishing is typically prohibited or limited within MPAs.

## INCOIS
Indian National Centre for Ocean Information Services. The primary government agency providing ocean information services including PFZ advisories, tsunami warnings, and ocean state forecasts.

## MOSDAC
Meteorological and Oceanographic Satellite Data Archival Centre, operated by ISRO. Provides satellite-derived ocean and atmospheric data including SST, ocean color, and wind fields.

## IMD
India Meteorological Department. Issues weather forecasts, cyclone warnings, and marine weather bulletins for Indian waters.

## Ocean Current
The continuous, directed movement of ocean water. Currents affect vessel speed (following a current increases speed, opposing it decreases speed) and fish distribution.

## Tide
The periodic rise and fall of sea levels caused by gravitational forces of the Moon and Sun. Important for harbor entry/exit timing and nearshore fishing.

## Provenance
In ORCA, provenance refers to the metadata tracking where data came from, when it was retrieved, and its reliability tier. Every piece of evidence in ORCA carries provenance information.
