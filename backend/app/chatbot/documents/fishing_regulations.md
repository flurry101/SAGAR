# Fishing Regulations — India

## Marine Protected Areas (MPAs)

Marine Protected Areas are designated zones where fishing activities are restricted or prohibited to protect marine biodiversity. In India, MPAs include:

- **National Marine Parks:** Gulf of Mannar National Marine Park, Mahatma Gandhi Marine National Park (Andaman)
- **Wildlife Sanctuaries:** Gulf of Kutch Marine National Park
- **Biosphere Reserves:** Gulf of Mannar Biosphere Reserve

### Rules
- Fishing is prohibited within the core zone of most MPAs.
- Buffer zones may allow limited fishing with permits.
- Violating MPA boundaries can result in fines and vessel seizure.

## Seasonal Fishing Bans

India enforces seasonal fishing bans to protect breeding populations:

### East Coast (Bay of Bengal)
- **Ban period:** April 15 to June 14 (61 days)
- **Applies to:** Mechanized fishing vessels

### West Coast (Arabian Sea)
- **Ban period:** June 1 to July 31 (61 days)
- **Applies to:** Mechanized fishing vessels

### Key Rules
- Non-motorized traditional craft are typically exempt.
- Motorized boats under certain tonnage may be exempt in some states.
- State-specific variations exist — check local fisheries department.

# Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ)

- India's EEZ extends 200 nautical miles from the coastline.
- Indian fishers must not cross into another country's EEZ.
- The International Maritime Boundary Line (IMBL) with Sri Lanka is particularly sensitive.
- ORCA checks EEZ boundaries as part of geofence evaluation.

## Vessel Registration

- All mechanized fishing vessels must be registered with the state fisheries department.
- Registration includes vessel specifications (length, beam width, engine power).
- ORCA uses registered vessel specifications for safety calculations.
